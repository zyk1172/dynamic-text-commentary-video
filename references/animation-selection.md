# Animation selection

Use the bundled Microsoft Fluent Emoji assets and animate their entrance, drift and scale with Remotion frames. Score catalog items by tag overlap with scene narration, then emotion, difference from the prior selection, duration fit, and preferred position. For legal, news, labour and social commentary, prefer symbols and objects; use cat or duck only when they clarify a human feeling. Do not repeat the same asset in adjacent scenes when another relevant asset exists.
